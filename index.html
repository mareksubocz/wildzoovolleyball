Privacy Policy

Wild Zoo Volleyball · last updated 19 October 2026

Wild Zoo Volleyball is made by Programaro. This page explains what the game collects, why, and how to get it deleted. Questions: mrksubocz+programaro@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • A device identifier. The game creates your account automatically from your device's identifier, so you never need to sign up or give an email address.
  • Your player ID and your in-game name.
  • Google Play Games, if you're signed in to it on Android: your Play Games player ID, so your account comes back when you reinstall the game or switch phones, and your Play Games name and profile picture link. If your account has no name yet, your Play Games name becomes your in-game name; you can change it in your profile.
  • Friends and parties: who you've added as a friend and who you play with.
  • Game progress: match results, your rating, your place on the rankings and the animals you own.
  • Feedback you send: your message, any screenshots you attach, and the game version, device model, operating system version and screen you were on, to help us fix bugs.
  • Your IP address, which our servers need to connect you to matches.

We don't show ads, don't use analytics or tracking tools, and don't sell or share your data for marketing. All connections between the game and our servers are encrypted.

Who can see it

  • Other players see your name, player ID, rating and ranking.
  • Service providers that run the game for us: our game server is hosted by UpCloud in Frankfurt, Germany; connections to it pass through Cloudflare, which sees your IP address; and live match traffic is relayed by GD-Sync, which sees your IP address and in-game actions while you play.
  • Google, if you use Google Play Games sign-in, under Google's privacy policy.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, everything listed above is deleted with it.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account inside the game, or by email if you no longer have the game installed. See Delete your account.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, by emailing mrksubocz+programaro@gmail.com. If you are in the EU or UK you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

The game is not aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
