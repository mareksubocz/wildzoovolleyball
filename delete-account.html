Delete your account

Wild Zoo Volleyball by Programaro

In the game

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Without the game

Email mrksubocz+programaro@gmail.com and include your player ID (shown under your name in your profile in the game) or the name you use in the game.

We'll delete your account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account, player ID and name
  • Your friends list and party memberships
  • Your match results, rating and rankings
  • The animals you own
  • Feedback and screenshots you sent

What is kept

Nothing linked to you. Deletion can't be undone. If you play again later, you'll start with a new, empty account.
